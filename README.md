## 🛠️ Things I'm building

### ⌨️ KeyFlip

A little GNOME utility that lets you disable your laptop's built-in keyboard without affecting external keyboards.

I made it because I use a mechanical keyboard with my laptop and kept accidentally pressing the keyboard underneath it 😭

It started as a tiny script and somehow became an actual Linux app.

### 🎥 LIVE Loop

A companion app for livestreamers that helps keep a stream moving.

It can remind you to reset for new viewers, explain what's happening, ask chat a question, recap the goal and generally avoid those moments where your brain goes completely blank mid-stream.

### ✍️ Writing experiments

I'm also playing around with distraction-free writing tools.

I really like software that gets out of the way and lets you actually do the thing you opened it for.
